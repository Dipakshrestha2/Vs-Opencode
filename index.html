About Pragma Montessori Home

Located in Bastolathar, Pokhara-9, Pragma Montessori Home is a premier private educational institution focused entirely on early childhood development. We follow the authentic Montessori method to ensure every child gets the tailored guidance they need to shine.

Our Leadership

Guided by pioneers in Montessori education in Gandaki Province

JK

Mrs. Janaki Khadka

Founder & Principal

Mrs. Janaki Khadka is a pioneer in early childhood education, recognized as the first Montessori teacher trainer in Gandaki Province (since 2063 B.S.). Under her leadership, Pragma Montessori Home sets the standard for early education in Pokhara.
